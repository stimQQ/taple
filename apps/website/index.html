Features

Record it. Polish it. Export it.

Made for product demos, walkthroughs and tutorials, from the first take to the finished video, in one app on your Mac.

  • 01

    Screen, window or area

    Show exactly what matters: record the whole screen, a window or an area, together with your camera, microphone and system audio.

  • 02

    Edit right away

    The editor opens when you stop recording. Trim out the mistakes and zoom in on the details.

  • 03

    Make it look polished

    Give every demo a clean frame: style the background, padding, rounded corners and shadow.

  • 04

    AI captions on your Mac

    Caption your tutorials with Whisper, running right on your Mac.

  • 05

    Noise reduction

    Clean up your voiceover with DeepFilterNet noise reduction.

  • 06

    MP4, GIF or clipboard

    Export your demo to MP4 or GIF, or copy it straight to your clipboard.

  • 07

    Built-in teleprompter

    Follow your script on the built-in teleprompter while you walk through the product.

  • 08

    Speaks your language

    The interface follows your system language: English or Chinese.

AI Edit

Let AI make the first cut.

AI Edit reads what you said and checks what happened on screen, then tightens the video. Every change lands on the timeline for you to review.

  • 09

    Tight pacing

    Cuts filler words and long pauses, but never a moment where you click, type or the screen changes.

  • 10

    Zooms and captions

    Zooms in on the details that matter and adds captions, so viewers can follow every step.

  • 11

    You stay in control

    Each change shows up on the timeline. Keep it, adjust it or undo it.

  • 12

    Works with your AI agent

    Connect Claude Code, Codex, Claude Desktop or Cursor in one click, then edit your recordings by chatting with your agent.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else you want to know before you record your next demo or tutorial? Email hello@taple.app or open an issue on GitHub.

Is Taple free?

Yes. Taple is free to download and use, with no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. Taple has no accounts. Download it, open it and start recording.

Where are my recordings stored?

Only on your Mac. Taple stores your recordings, projects and captions locally, and nowhere else.

Do captions need an internet connection?

Only the first time. Captions are generated on your Mac with Whisper. The first time you use them, Taple downloads the Whisper model from Hugging Face.

Does AI Edit cost anything?

Taple itself is free. AI Edit uses your own API key from apimodels.app, Anthropic or OpenAI, and that provider charges you for what you use. Everything else in Taple works without a key.

What does AI Edit send?

The transcript, which Taple makes on your Mac, still frames from the recording and the timing of your clicks go to the AI provider you choose. Your video and audio files are not uploaded.

Which AI agents work with Taple?

Claude Code, Codex, Claude Desktop and Cursor. Open Settings, choose AI Edit and connect your agent in one click. Taple must be installed on the same Mac. Your agent can open any recording and edit it while you watch the timeline.

Which Macs are supported?

Any Mac running macOS 13.1 or later, with Apple silicon or an Intel processor. There’s a separate download for each.

Not sure which one you have? Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. A Mac with Apple silicon lists a “Chip”, an Intel Mac lists a “Processor”.

Is it open source?

Yes. Taple is open source under the GNU AGPLv3. The source code is on GitHub, where you can also report issues.

How do updates work?

Taple checks for updates by requesting the latest release information from GitHub. Every version is published on the releases page, and the download buttons on this site always point to the latest one.
